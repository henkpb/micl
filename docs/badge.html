Anchored

Give the element the badge belongs to an anchor-name and point the badge at it with position-anchor. The badge is hidden from assistive technology, so the element's accessible name carries its information.

Trailing

A trailing badge needs no anchor: it is laid out in the flow, vertically centered, with a small gap after the label.

In navigation

Navigation items anchor their badges automatically. See them on the navigation bar and navigation rail pages, where an expanded rail moves large badges behind the label.
