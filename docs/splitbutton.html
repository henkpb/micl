Split buttons

One button doing two jobs: the wide half performs the obvious action, the narrow half opens a menu of the less obvious ones. Open the menu below and watch the seam round off.

Styles Sizes The menu

Styles

Both halves take the same button style. Material Design defines four of them for a split button — the text style is not among them, as its seam would be invisible.

The leading half takes a leading icon like any other button, and either half can be disabled on its own

Sizes

Five sizes, matching the Button. The seam between the halves grows with them: nearly square at the small end, generously rounded at the large end.

The menu

The menu is an ordinary Menu placed inside the split button, opened by the native popovertarget attribute. While it is open the trailing half rounds into a pill, takes a state layer and turns its chevron over — all in CSS.
