Navigation rails

A navigation rail houses destinations and functionality for the application. Pick a variant — the rail appears at the edge of this page, and the modal variants open from the menu button in the top bar.

  • Collapsed & expanded
  • Modal on demand
  • Adaptive from 840 px

Variants

The adaptive variant behaves as a modal rail on narrow windows and becomes a standing expanded rail from 840 px.
