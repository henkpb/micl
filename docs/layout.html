Foundation

Layout

CSS classes that implement the Material Design 3 layout guidelines: a window with safe margins, a rail region for navigation, and a pane area divided into one to three panes that stack or hide at the Material breakpoints.

  • Fixed & flexible panes
  • Split-pane layout
  • Adaptive breakpoints
Window Panes Columns Adaptive Theming

Window

Add micl-window to the <body>, put the navigation in the rail region and the content in a <main class="micl-panes">. The frame below is a window of its own, so its panes respond to the frame's width.

Resize the browser to see the panes stack and hide: open the window demo.

Panes

A micl-pane is flexible and adapts to its content; micl-pane--fixed is 360px wide at the expanded breakpoint and 412px from the large breakpoint, and sized by its content below that.

Fixed and flexible

Flexible panes shared evenly

Columns within a pane

Divide a pane with micl-pane__columns and micl-pane__column. A column grows by default; micl-pane__column--fixed keeps the width of its content.

Adaptive panes

These classes follow the window's width, not the width of their container, so try them in the window demo. micl-panes--split makes two flexible panes meet at the horizontal centre of the window, keeping the spacer over a foldable's hinge.

  • micl-panes--stacked-to-medium, -expanded, -large, -extralarge: stack the panes below that breakpoint
  • micl-pane--hidden-to-medium, -expanded, -large, -extralarge: hide a pane below that breakpoint
  • micl-pane__columns--stacked-to-medium, -expanded, -large, -extralarge: stack columns below that breakpoint
