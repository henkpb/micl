Bottom sheets

Bottom sheets show secondary content anchored to the bottom of the screen.

  • Resizable via drag handle
  • Light-dismiss popover
  • Height managed for you

Showcase

Click a button to open one of the MICL bottom sheets. The resizable ones change height when their drag-handle is activated, or when the area around it is dragged.
